# The Trail

Eight miles, one footbridge, a meadow, and a climb. Along the way the chefs
need to know how much water is left, how far there is to go, what the sign
says, and where Rémy is in the guidebook.

## What you'll learn

- Two ways to show a fraction of something: `Gauge` and its one-row cousin
  `LineGauge`.
- The split between a widget and its state, and `render_stateful_widget`,
  which is how `List`, `Scrollbar` and `Table` remember where you are.
- How `Paragraph` handles text that does not fit: wrapping, alignment and
  scrolling.

## Exercises

| Exercise | What it covers |
