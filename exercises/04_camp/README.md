# Camp

A fire ring in a stand of pines. The fire burns down, dinner is served, and
three days blur into one.

## What you'll learn

- `Sparkline`, the smallest chart there is.
- `Table`, which is a `List` with columns, and how it borrows `Constraint`
  from `Layout` to size them.
- `Tabs`, which draws the bar and leaves what goes under it to you.

## Exercises

| Exercise | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| `lesson13` | A `Sparkline` of the fire through the evening. |
| `lesson14` | The meal plan as a `Table` with widths and a header `Row`. |
| `lesson15` | One tab per day with `Tabs` and `select`. |
