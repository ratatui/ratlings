# Planning

The pack is by the door. Around the kitchen table, the chefs work out when
they are going, where, and how far up. Then, on the threshold, somebody
remembers the matches.

## What you'll learn

- That a widget can draw more than text: a calendar, a map, and a chart are
  all just widgets with a bit more configuration.
- The idea of a coordinate system that is not the terminal grid, and how
  `Canvas` and `Chart` map one onto the other.
- That widgets draw on top of what is already there, and why a popup needs
  `Clear` underneath it.

## Exercises

| Exercise | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| `lesson4` | Marking days on a `Monthly` calendar with a `CalendarEventStore`. |
| `lesson5` | Drawing the route on a `Canvas` with `Line` shapes and labels. |
| `lesson6` | Plotting the elevation profile with `Chart`, `Dataset` and `Axis`. |
| `lesson7` | Floating a popup over a busy screen with `Clear`. |
